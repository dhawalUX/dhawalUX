## Hey, I'm Dhawal 👋

I'm a **senior product designer** who lives in the gap between design and engineering — I design systems, mobile apps, and enterprise products, and then I go all the way into the frontend and **ship them in code, not just screens.**

Right now I'm building the vendor experience at **StyleMatch** — helping Indian clothing vendors price for a US market they can't see — where I own the design and ship the core flows in React myself.

- 🎨 &nbsp;Design systems · enterprise & mobile · 0→1 products
- 💻 &nbsp;Designer who codes — I close the gap between the mockup and the merge
- 🌐 &nbsp;More work → **[dhawalwaykole.com](https://dhawalwaykole.com)**
